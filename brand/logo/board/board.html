The brief

A personal mark for a portfolio site in a Little Nightmares "Pale City" art direction: a near-black blue page, one cold light source, one rare warm accent, institutional textures. The mark sits small in the site header and as the favicon, and it has to hold its own against a dark, grainy page without turning into a smudge.

structuralquietexactinstitutionalunsettling

Success: reads at 16 px in a browser tab, carries the idea in one colour, holds weight reversed on a near-black page, and is not another initial in a stock font.

The mark

The Decision, Opened by Light

A flowchart decision diamond cut open by a vertical slot of light that reads as an I.

  • The diamond is the one shape every process map shares - the point where a business decides - so the mark says what the work is without drawing a brain, a node or a chart.
  • The slot is not a letter drawn on top; it is an absence. On the dark page it is the only lit thing in the frame, which is exactly how the reference art works.
  • Two straight-edged forms, four angles, no curve to go lumpy: it survives 16 px and one colour, and it is still an I at a glance.
  • Three cuts, not one: the display mark, a small-size cut with a wider slot, and a reversed cut thinned against light bloom.
  • It seeds the system - the slot becomes the lit column behind a photo, the diamond becomes a bullet and a section marker.

Notes

Bone #D9D5C8 on void #06080C is the primary pair - 13.65:1, measured. Void on white is the print and light-mode pair, 20.04:1. Brass #D9A441 is a hover state on the dark page only (8.91:1); on white it is 2.25:1, so it is never the mark on a light background.

  • Install symbol-small as the site icon and favicon, the horizontal lockup in the header.
  • Keep the reversed cut for anything light-on-dark; do not reverse the display cut.
  • Run a professional trademark search before any commercial use.
